## Overview

3D product renders of a PUMA bowling-style handbag that pairs black leather with fluffy rose-textured panels. Modelled, textured and lit in Blender, then rendered as multi-angle product shots, six orthographic views, close-ups and a 360° spin.

## Highlights

- **Fur against leather**: hair and material settings bring out the volume of the rose-shaped fluff, set against the sheen and stitching of the leather
- **Every angle covered**: one model renders front, back, left, right, top and bottom views plus styled angles, ready for e-commerce and social use
- **Close-ups**: zips, hardware, handles and the fluffy logo, shown up close

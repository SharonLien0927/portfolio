## Overview

A product film for a new LUXGEN model. I handled the motion storyboard, scenes, animation, rendering and compositing, including the small hand, foot and phone interactions. The car model, captions, music and the closing colour change were done by other team members.

## Pipeline

Shots and pacing were locked in a previs storyboard. I then built the scenes, rendered in Maya and brought the beauty, AO, Z-depth and mask passes into After Effects for compositing and grading. Rendering in passes meant light, depth of field and local colour could be adjusted in post without re-rendering.

## Spin-off version

The same scenes and pipeline were reused for the GPM Welcab accessible version, keeping the brand look consistent across models.

## Overview

360° exterior and interior views of the Altis and Camry for the Toyota website, letting visitors switch colours and spin the car. I also modelled the Rav4 wheels.

## Key work

- Fixed materials and missing parts on the source car models, built extra geometry where needed, and created the paint shader for each colour
- Rendered multi-colour, multi-angle image sequences from fixed cameras in Maya + Redshift
- Retouched leather sheen, mirror reflections and overall grade in Photoshop so every colour looks consistent on the site

## Overview

3D assets for the Play UR series by CIPU, our group’s bag brand, used in Instagram and other social ads. There were no existing 3D files, so I photographed the real bags and modelled them from scratch in Blender.

## Highlights

- **True-to-life detail**: body, pockets, webbing and hardware matched against photos of the real product, down to stitching and fabric folds
- **One model, many uses**: the same model drives two colourways (milk tea and green), multi-angle product shots, orthographic views and 360° spins
- **Social animation**: a 9:16 vertical film where cute characters roam the city and a cinema to show the series’ playful side, with the animation timing tuned by hand

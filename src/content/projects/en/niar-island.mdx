## Overview

An interactive website where themed zones sit across a low-poly 3D island. Every zone is full of small animations, so three artists split the work; I was responsible for the mountains and buildings.

## My role

I handled my zones from discussion and design through modelling, animation, rendering and compositing, coordinated style and transitions with the other artists, and launched with the web engineers.

## Overview

SURREALM is a multi-user VR teaching product launched in June 2021, built for teaching and learning and tailored into different business packages for each client. I created its convenience store and café scenes, which use VR’s immersion to give companies a realistic training space without disrupting day-to-day operations.

## My role

I handled style development, low-poly modelling, texturing, and tuning and lighting in Unity. I produced almost all of the product’s art, with a small part from other artists.

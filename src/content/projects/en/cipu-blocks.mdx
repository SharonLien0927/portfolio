## Overview

A 9:16 vertical brand animation for CIPU’s Building Blocks bag series, made for Instagram ads.

## Concept

Echoing the series’ block pattern, pastel cubes fall, spin and stack until they form the backpack itself. Going from blocks to finished bag ties the pattern to the product directly, and catches a scrolling thumb within seconds.

## Overview

A product film for the LUXGEN S5. Working under an art director, I handled the motion storyboard, materials, scenes, lighting, animation and After Effects compositing.

## Technical notes

- Paint, metal, leather and lamp materials matched to the real car so close-ups hold up
- Lighting and compositing bring out the body lines and interior details, showing off the car’s quality and tech

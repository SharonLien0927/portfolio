## Overview

A naked-eye 3D film for an Ingrasys Technology showcase. I took it from concept and storyboard through modelling, animation, rendering and compositing on my own, in three weeks.

## Concept

Water drives the whole piece: it surges against the screen frame, churns inside it and reveals the product and company logo, which then fly out towards the audience together — giving the product more impact and staying power.

## Technical notes

- Fluid and product animation built in Blender, with out-of-frame shots planned around the corner of the screen
- Light and pacing unified in After Effects for playback on the large display

## Overview

China Airlines used a large naked-eye 3D screen as the centrepiece of its travel fair booth. I handled the whole pipeline on my own — concept, storyboard, modelling, animation, rendering and compositing — and supported playback on site. The project took three weeks.

## Concept

The film opens on an aircraft window. As the shade lifts, the full screen is revealed, the globe turns, and landmarks from around the world rise one by one. The China Airlines logo and a plane then fly straight out at the audience — the feeling of setting off on a trip.

## Technical notes

- Camera set up to the real screen proportions and viewing angle so the out-of-frame illusion holds on site
- Scenes, animation and Cycles rendering in Blender; compositing and grading in After Effects
- Shots prioritised in the storyboard so key moments were locked first on a tight schedule

## Overview

An online museum for China Airlines, where visitors explore the airline’s history online and the client can upload videos and update content at any time. The site has an opening animation, a home page and five halls; I created the opening animation, the home page and the SHARE and TIME halls.

## My role

I took my areas from discussion and design through modelling, rendering and compositing, worked with other artists to keep one consistent look, and launched them with the web engineers.

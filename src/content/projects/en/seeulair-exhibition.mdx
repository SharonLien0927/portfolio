## Overview

seeulair is an online exhibition product developed in-house. I created most of its 3D exhibition scenes and took each one from design and modelling through rendering and publishing on my own. The scenes come in four spaces:

- **Round lobby**: a tall interior centred on a circular hall
- **Open gallery**: a premium, airy, light-filled interior
- **Showroom**: a tall, long interior
- **Specialty store**: an interior set up as a brand store

Each space comes in minimal, luxury, wood, sci-fi, colourful, organic and gaming styles, so users from any industry can find a fitting venue.

## Process

The schedule was tight, so scene production had to be much faster: I used AI image generation for quick style, layout and reference exploration, then modelled, lit and rendered in Blender — about four days from nothing to a finished scene.

## Design idea

Built for online exhibitions: plenty of blank walls for users to hang posters and videos, and open floor space for 3D models. Scenes are rendered as 720° viewpoints so visitors can walk through them on the site; the 360° videos below give a feel for the real experience.

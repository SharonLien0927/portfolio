## Overview

Two naked-eye 3D films for Foxconn: one for an event opening and one showcasing its electric vehicle. On both, I planned the scripts and led technical direction while team members handled modelling, animation and rendering.

## My role

- **Script & storyboard**: planned shots around the event theme and the corner screen, timing when and where objects break out of the frame
- **Technical direction**: set camera, proportion and render specs so the illusion held on the real screen, and reviewed and corrected the team’s work throughout
- **Scheduling**: split the work and tracked progress under exhibition deadlines so both films shipped on time

## Result

The EV film builds to the car driving out of the depth of the screen and bursting through the frame; the opening film draws the crowd with a ball that seems to pass through the screen.

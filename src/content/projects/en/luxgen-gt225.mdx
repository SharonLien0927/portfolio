## Overview

The LUXGEN GT225 product film followed the same previs → render → comp → final pipeline as GPM, with many more shots and much larger scenes. I handled the motion storyboard, scenes, animation, rendering and compositing; some captions, the opening effect, wheel compositing and the S5-to-GT225 transformation were done by other team members.

## Technical notes

- Pass-based rendering and After Effects compositing kept light and colour consistent across a large number of shots
- The before/after frames show how compositing lifts paint reflections, glow and mood

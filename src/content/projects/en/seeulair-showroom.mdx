## Overview

3D scenes for seeulair, an online exhibition product developed in-house. I designed, built, rendered and published every scene myself. This series is the Mid-size Showroom, made in minimal, luxury, wood, sci-fi, colourful, organic and gaming styles.

## Process

The schedule was tight, so scene production had to be fast: each scene started with quick style and layout exploration in Midjourney, then was modelled, lit and rendered in Blender — about four days from nothing to a finished scene.

## Design idea

Built for online exhibitions: plenty of blank walls for visitors to hang their own posters and videos, and open floor space for 3D models. Scenes are rendered as 720° viewpoints so visitors can walk through them on the site.

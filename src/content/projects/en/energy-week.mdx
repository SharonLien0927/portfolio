## Overview

An interactive site where five floating islands each present a theme, all built from low-poly 3D models. I created the opening animation and the main island (certification zone); other artists built the remaining islands and I integrated and polished the whole set.

## My role

I handled discussion, design, high- and low-poly modelling, animation, rendering and compositing for my parts, and launched the site with the web engineers. The live site has since been replaced, so the original version can be seen in the YouTube promo.

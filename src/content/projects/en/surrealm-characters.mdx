## Overview

Characters and teaching props for the SURREALM multi-user VR product. Another artist designed the characters, and I built them as 3D models with textures. The props are part of a classroom lesson: a Taiwan puzzle and county landmarks that help teachers introduce local culture.

## My role

Character modelling from concept art, prop design, low-poly modelling and texturing, and integration in Unity.

## Overview

An educational VR product from 2018 for the VR Focus standalone headset. It pairs maths lessons with escape-room gameplay so students enjoy learning while they solve puzzles. Two games were made.

## My role

I contributed to the script and style development, built the models and textures, then tuned and lit the scenes in Unity and worked with the programming team on integration.

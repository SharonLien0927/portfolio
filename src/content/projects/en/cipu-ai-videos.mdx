## Overview

A series of AI product videos for CIPU, our group’s bag brand, covering the Ailé, Airy, Light, Lissom and Luz II lines for Instagram and other social ads. I made every video from idea to final cut on my own — no 3D assets, just AI video generation and post-production.

## Process

1. **Script**: write the script around each product’s selling points and use cases
2. **Refine with AI**: talk the script and shots through with an AI assistant
3. **Storyboard**: storyboard with AI images or by hand to lock each frame’s composition
4. **Generate**: create the shots with Seedance or Kling from the storyboard
5. **Edit & score**: edit, pace and add music to finish the film

## Result

AI cut production time for product videos dramatically, while the script and storyboard kept every shot on-brand and focused on what sells the bag — storage, water resistance, commuting and travel.

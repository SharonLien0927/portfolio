## Overview

3D assets for the CIPU KEIRYO Light, used in social ads. The bag’s selling point is that it converts between a backpack and a shoulder bag, so showing that change clearly mattered as much as the look of the product.

## Highlights

- **Modelled from scratch**: built by hand in Blender from photos of the real bag, including the movable straps and buckles
- **Transformation animation**: the straps fold and the backpack becomes a shoulder bag, so viewers get the two-in-one design at a glance
- **Poses & 360°**: separate wearing-pose animations and 360° spins for both modes

Hi there,

I’m Sharon, a 3D designer and generalist with a degree in Digital Multimedia Design (game design track) from Shih Hsin University.

Since 2017 I’ve worked as a 3D artist at Surreal Technology and KingOne Design, building more than seven years of studio experience. My work spans naked-eye 3D billboards, automotive website renders and product films, educational VR games, online exhibitions and interactive web 3D, for brands including China Airlines, Foxconn, Toyota and LUXGEN.

What I do best is solve problems with 3D: understanding what a client and a project really need, choosing the right way to present it and the right pipeline to build it, and delivering the best result within real-world time and budget. I can take a project end to end on my own — concept, storyboard, modelling, animation, rendering and compositing — and in recent years I’ve increasingly taken the lead: writing scripts, providing technical direction and managing schedules so the team delivers on tight exhibition deadlines.

Alongside Blender, Unity, Substance Painter, Maya and After Effects, I keep adding new skills, including AI video production and web design in Framer, so 3D can reach video, websites and other media faster and more completely.

After work, crochet is how I unwind. It started with small pieces for myself and friends, and grew into the idea of taking commissions: in an age when digital tools and AI can generate almost anything in seconds, something made by hand, one stitch at a time, feels more precious than ever. I’d love to make that warmth into something you can keep.

I’d love to hear about your project.
